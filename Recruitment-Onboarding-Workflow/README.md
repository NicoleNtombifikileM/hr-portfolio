# HR Recruitment and Onboarding Workflow

## Project Overview
This project demonstrates a structured recruitment and onboarding process designed to help an HR team recruit suitable candidates, maintain accurate documentation, and ensure that new employees are prepared for work.

## Project Objectives
- Organise recruitment activities from vacancy approval to candidate selection.
- Track candidate progress through each recruitment stage.
- Ensure required employment documents are collected and checked.
- Support a consistent onboarding experience for new employees.

## Recruitment Process
1. Receive and confirm the vacancy request.
2. Advertise the position and receive applications.
3. Screen CVs against the job requirements.
4. Shortlist suitable candidates.
5. Conduct interviews and assessments.
6. Complete reference and document checks where applicable.
7. Confirm the selection and prepare the employment offer.

## Onboarding Process
1. Collect and verify required employee documents.
2. Prepare the employment contract.
3. Complete employee registration and HR records.
4. Arrange induction and explain workplace policies.
5. Confirm role requirements, reporting lines, and site allocation.
6. Track outstanding documents and probation review dates.

## Example Tracking Statuses
- Vacancy Requested
- Recruiting
- Interview Stage
- Selected
- Documents Pending
- Ready for Onboarding
- Onboarding Completed

## HR Value
A standard workflow improves record keeping, recruitment visibility, compliance follow-up, communication, and the new employee experience.

## Data Privacy
This is a demonstration project. Use fictional candidate information only. Do not upload real CVs, identity numbers, contact details, employment contracts, or confidential company information.
